Privacy

Last updated November 23, 2026.

Who uses SUZY

Only her owner, Allan Mukendi. SUZY isn't offered to anyone else, and nobody else can sign in to her.

What SUZY accesses

Her owner's own Google Drive, and within it only the folder SUZY Link, which she uses to pass signed notes, messages and updates between her owner's own computers and phone.

What she does with it

Nothing beyond that. SUZY doesn't sell, share, publish or advertise with any data, and doesn't send it to anyone other than her owner's own devices. Google user data is used only to provide SUZY's features to her owner, in line with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Where data is kept

On her owner's own devices and in his own Google Drive. Removing SUZY's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions stops it at once.
